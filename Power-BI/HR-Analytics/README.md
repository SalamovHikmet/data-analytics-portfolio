# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes HR employee data using Power BI to understand workforce composition and employee attrition patterns.

The dashboard provides insights into employee demographics, departments, salary ranges, job roles, job satisfaction, and work experience to help identify patterns associated with employee attrition.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization

## Key Analysis

- Total and active employee analysis
- Employee attrition rate
- Attrition by department
- Attrition by salary range
- Attrition by gender
- Attrition by total work experience
- Employee age group distribution
- Department-wise employee distribution
- Job role and job satisfaction analysis

## Data Source

The project uses a CSV dataset containing employee-level HR data.

The dataset includes information such as employee age, department, gender, job role, job satisfaction, monthly income, salary range, work experience, overtime, performance rating, and employee attrition.

## Key Insights

- The analyzed workforce contains approximately 1,417 employees, of whom around 1,186 are active employees.
- A total of 231 employee attrition cases were identified, representing an attrition rate of approximately 16.3%.
- The average employee age is approximately 36.9 years.
- Employees have an average of approximately 7 years of experience based on the dashboard analysis.
- The 26–35 age group represents the largest employee segment.
- Operations has the largest workforce among the analyzed departments.
- Male employees account for approximately 63.2% of recorded attrition cases.
- Attrition cases are more concentrated in the lower salary ranges.
- Attrition is also more concentrated among employees with lower levels of work experience.
